# ECMAScript 语法规则：statement

```antlr
statement : block
    | variableStatement
    | importStatement
    | exportStatement
    | emptyStatement_
    | classDeclaration
    | functionDeclaration
    | expressionStatement
    | ifStatement
    | iterationStatement
    | continueStatement
    | breakStatement
    | returnStatement
    | yieldStatement
    | withStatement
    | labelledStatement
    | switchStatement
    | throwStatement
    | tryStatement
    | debuggerStatement ;
```
