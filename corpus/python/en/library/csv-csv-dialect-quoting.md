# Dialect.quoting

Controls when quotes should be generated by the writer and recognised by the
reader.  It can take on any of the `QUOTE_\* constants`
and defaults to `QUOTE_MINIMAL` if *quotechar* is not `None`,
and `QUOTE_NONE` otherwise.
