# ScrolledText

The keyword arguments are passed to the `~tkinter.Text` widget.

When *use_ttk* is true, the surrounding frame and the scroll bar are the
themed `tkinter.ttk` widgets;
the default is the classic `tkinter` widgets.

> *Changed in next*: Added the *use_ttk* parameter.
