# LOG_REMOTEAUTH

Facilities, depending on availability in `<syslog.h>` for `LOG_AUTHPRIV`,
`LOG_FTP`, `LOG_NETINFO`, `LOG_REMOTEAUTH`,
`LOG_INSTALL` and `LOG_RAS`.

> *Changed in 3.13*: Added :const:`LOG_FTP`, :const:`LOG_NETINFO`, :const:`LOG_REMOTEAUTH`, :const:`LOG_INSTALL`, :const:`LOG_RAS`, and :const:`LOG_LAUNCHD`.
