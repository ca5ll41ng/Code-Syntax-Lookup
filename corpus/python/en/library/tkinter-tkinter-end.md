# END

Symbolic indices used by the `Text`, `Entry`, `Listbox`
and `Canvas` widgets, such as `'insert'` (the insertion cursor),
`'current'`, `'end'`, `'anchor'` and the bounds of the selection
(`'sel.first'` and `'sel.last'`).
