# int_info

A `named tuple` that holds information about Python's internal
representation of integers.  The attributes are read only.

attribute:: int_info.bits_per_digit

attribute:: int_info.sizeof_digit

attribute:: int_info.default_max_str_digits

attribute:: int_info.str_digits_check_threshold

> *Added in 3.1*

> *Changed in 3.11*: Added :attr:`~int_info.default_max_str_digits` and :attr:`~int_info.str_digits_check_threshold`.
