# Integral

Subtypes `Rational` and adds a conversion to `int`.  Provides
defaults for `float`, `~Rational.numerator`, and
`~Rational.denominator`.  Adds abstract methods for `pow` with
modulus and bit-string operations: `<<`, `>>`, `&`, `^`, `|`,
`~`.
