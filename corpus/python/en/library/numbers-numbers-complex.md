# Complex

Subclasses of this type describe complex numbers and include the operations
that work on the built-in `complex` type. These are: conversions to
`complex` and `bool`, `.real`, `.imag`, `+`,
`-`, `*`, `/`, `**`, `abs`, `conjugate`, `==`, and
`!=`. All except `-` and `!=` are abstract.
