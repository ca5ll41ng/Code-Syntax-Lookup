# SIO_RCVALL

Constants for Windows' WSAIoctl(). The constants are used as arguments to the
`~socket.socket.ioctl` method of socket objects.

> *Changed in 3.6*: ``SIO_LOOPBACK_FAST_PATH`` was added.
